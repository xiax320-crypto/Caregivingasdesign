About

This project explores how everyday people think and act like designers without realizing it. Through four interviews, we discovered that design exists in daily life — in cooking, organizing, cleaning, and adapting spaces.

Interviews

Cooking as Design

Li Wei describes cooking as balancing flavor, color, and emotion.

“Cooking is creating an experience for others.”

Organizing Space

Chen Mei adjusts space based on how people live.

“It’s about comfort, not just storage.”

Student Life

Alex constantly redesigns his small apartment.

“It never feels finished.”

Cleaning

Maria sees cleaning as changing atmosphere.

“It changes how people feel.”

What We Learned

Design is Everyday

People design without realizing it.

Design is Iteration

Everything improves through adjustment.

Design is Emotion

It shapes feelings, not just function.

Reflection

At first, we focused on functionality. But interviews showed that people care more about experience and feeling.

Many participants did not see themselves as designers, even though they made design decisions.

This project changed how we understand design — it is part of everyday life.
